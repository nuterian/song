yourself.

Change a shot. Move a moment. Or just ask.

By hand.Shots, moments and words sit on one timeline. Drag them. The video follows in about a second.

By asking.Say it in words. A model on your own Mac proposes the change, and you decide.

To keep.Export in 1080p at 60 frames a second, with the words burned in.

Free. Open.
On your Mac.

No account, no uploads, no keys. Everything runs where your music is.
