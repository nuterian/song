Make your own Watch the whole song

First, it listens.

Then everything moves.

The kick swells the Sun. The planets follow its pull.

Three cameras.
One song.

Then direct it
yourself.

Change a shot. Move a moment. Or just ask.

  1. By hand. Shots, moments and words on one timeline. Drag them.
  2. By asking. A model on your Mac proposes the change. You decide.
  3. To keep. 1080p at 60, wide or tall, or a loop for Spotify's Canvas.

Free. Open.
On your Mac.

No account, no uploads, no keys. Everything runs where your music is.

It runs on a Mac with Apple Silicon.

# macOS, Apple Silicon git clone https://github.com/nuterian/song cd song && ./setup.sh visuals/.venv/bin/python -m visuals studio

Python 3.12 and ffmpeg · 2.1 GB, plus about 8 GB of models · about 12 minutes a song on an M4 Air · MIT, the lyric model non‑commercial
